AhaBox — Privacy Policy

App: AhaBox (Android package: com.thanhcs.fasterthinking) · Developer: Meli Labs · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile app AhaBox ("AhaBox", "the app"), published on Google Play and the App Store by Meli Labs ("Meli Labs", "we", "our", or "us"). Meli Labs is the developer of AhaBox and is responsible for the personal data processed through the app.

AhaBox is a daily habit, focus and brain-training app: mini games, water and habit reminders, XP and rewards. This policy explains what data AhaBox collects, how Meli Labs uses it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • If you sign in to AhaBox with Email, Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that sign-in provider.
  • If you use AhaBox as a Guest, an anonymous account is created. We do not collect your name or email.
  • A unique user ID is generated to store and sync your progress.

App data you create

  • XP, streaks, game records, favorites, habit and water-tracking entries, reminder times and app settings. Most of this is stored on your device; if you sign in, it is also synced to your account so you can restore it.

Device and usage information

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID
  • App interaction and usage events
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • App version, device model and operating system version
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AhaBox and its features
  • To save and sync your progress, XP and settings across devices
  • To send reminders and notifications you turn on (for example water or habit reminders)
  • To understand usage, fix bugs and improve AhaBox
  • To show advertisements

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Analytics

AhaBox uses Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how the app is used. Firebase may collect device information, usage data and performance data.

4. Advertising

AhaBox uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, which may be personalized. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, AhaBox sends reminders and app updates via Firebase Cloud Messaging and local notifications. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

6. Tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, AhaBox may ask permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, your advertising identifier may be used for personalized ads; if you decline, it will not be used for tracking.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google LLC) – Authentication, data sync, Analytics, Cloud Messaging
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) – Advertising
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) – Authentication
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) – Authentication

These providers process data under their own privacy policies, for example the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Data Sharing

Meli Labs shares data only with the service providers listed above to operate AhaBox, or when required by law. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Data Retention

We keep account and progress data while your AhaBox account is active. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of the providers above.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your AhaBox account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Your account and synced data are deleted immediately. You can also email nguyenvanthanh.cs94@gmail.com with the subject "AhaBox – Delete my account"; Meli Labs will delete your account and data within 30 days. Full instructions: https://thankvn.github.io/brain-test-privacy/delete-account/.

11. Children's Privacy

AhaBox is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
