AhaBox — Privacy Policy

App: AhaBox (iOS and Android; Android package: com.thanhcs.fasterthinking) · Developer: Meli Labs · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile app AhaBox ("AhaBox", "the app"), published on Google Play and the App Store by Meli Labs ("Meli Labs", "we", "our", or "us"). Meli Labs is the developer of AhaBox and is responsible for the personal data processed through the app.

AhaBox is a daily brain-training, focus and habit app with Aha, our mascot: brain games, focus sessions, water and habit tracking, XP, Sparks and streaks. This policy covers both the iOS and the Android app; where they differ (for example, ads exist only on Android) we say so. It explains what data AhaBox collects, how Meli Labs uses it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • If you sign in to AhaBox with Google or Apple (the only sign-in options), we receive your name, email address and, for Google, your profile photo from that provider. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email and name; if you do, we store Apple's private relay email and show you as "AhaBox Explorer".
  • If you use AhaBox as a Guest, an anonymous account is created. We do not collect your name or email.
  • A unique user ID is generated to store and sync your progress.

App data you create

  • XP, Sparks, streaks, game records, favorites, focus sessions, habit and water-tracking entries, reminder times and app settings. All of this is stored on your device.
  • If you use an account (Google, Apple or Guest), only the following is synced to your account in Firebase so you can restore it: your profile (display name, email, photo), your XP and level, your Sparks balance and rewards, your sound and haptics settings, sign-in timestamps, and your push notification token. Game records, favorites, focus sessions, habit and water entries and reminder times are not synced; they stay on your device.

Device and usage information

  • App interaction and usage events (Firebase Analytics)
  • App-launch diagnostics (Expo Insights): app version, operating system version and an anonymous, randomly generated install ID
  • App version, device model and operating system version
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications
  • The advertising ID (Android only; the iOS app does not access the advertising identifier, IDFA)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AhaBox and its features
  • To save and sync your progress, XP and settings across devices
  • To send reminders and notifications you turn on (for example daily reminders, water, habit or timer reminders)
  • To understand usage, fix bugs and improve AhaBox
  • To show advertisements (Android only)

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Analytics

AhaBox uses Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are opened and which features are used. On iOS, Firebase Analytics runs without the advertising identifier (IDFA), ad-ID collection is disabled, and the data is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites.

AhaBox also uses Expo Insights (Expo / 650 Industries, Inc.) to count app launches. It sends the app version, operating system version and an anonymous install ID that is generated on your device and is not linked to your account or identity.

4. Advertising

Android only: the Android app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, which may be personalized. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

iOS: the iOS app does not show ads and does not load AdMob or any other advertising service.

5. Notifications

AhaBox uses notifications only for things you turn on:

  • Daily reminders (a gentle nudge from Aha, at most once a day) are off by default. You can turn them on or off at any time in Settings → Daily reminders.
  • Water, habit and custom reminders, and focus or timer alerts, are sent only when you set them up in the related feature, and can be turned off there.

These are local notifications scheduled on your device. If you allow notifications, a push token is also registered with Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can deliver important service messages. AhaBox does not send advertising or marketing notifications. You can also turn off all notifications in your device settings (on iOS: Settings → Notifications → AhaBox).

6. No Tracking on iOS

On iOS, AhaBox does not track you across other companies' apps and websites and does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). Your data is not used for advertising and is not shared with data brokers.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google LLC) – Authentication, data sync, Analytics, Cloud Messaging
  • Expo Insights (650 Industries, Inc.) – Anonymous app-launch diagnostics
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) – Advertising (Android only)
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) – Authentication
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) – Authentication

These providers process data under their own privacy policies, for example the Google Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy and the Expo Privacy Policy.

8. Data Sharing

Meli Labs shares data only with the service providers listed above to operate AhaBox, or when required by law. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Data Retention

We keep account and progress data while your AhaBox account is active. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of the providers above.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your AhaBox account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Your account and synced data are deleted immediately, and all AhaBox data on that device (progress, Sparks, favorites, habits, water entries, reminders and scheduled notifications) is erased. Internal summary copies used by our admin dashboard are removed within 24 hours. You can also email nguyenvanthanh.cs94@gmail.com with the subject "AhaBox – Delete my account"; Meli Labs will delete your account and data within 30 days. Full instructions: https://thankvn.github.io/brain-test-privacy/delete-account/.

11. Children's Privacy

AhaBox is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
