Delete your AhaBox account

App: AhaBox (iOS and Android; Android package: com.thanhcs.fasterthinking) · Developer: Meli Labs · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page explains how users of AhaBox, an app developed by Meli Labs, can delete their AhaBox account and the data associated with it.

Option 1: Delete inside the AhaBox app

  1. Open AhaBox and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  4. Sign in again with Google or Apple when asked, to confirm it is you.

Your account and synced data are deleted immediately, and all AhaBox data on the device where you delete is erased. The app returns to a signed-out state, like a fresh install.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email Meli Labs at nguyenvanthanh.cs94@gmail.com with the subject "AhaBox – Delete my account", from the email address linked to your AhaBox account. Meli Labs will verify the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: the Firebase Authentication user (name, email, profile photo, user ID). If you used Sign in with Apple, the app's Apple sign-in token is also revoked.
  • Your cloud data: your user record (users/{your user ID}), which holds your profile (display name, email, photo), XP and level, Sparks balance and rewards, sound and haptics settings, sign-in timestamps and push notification tokens. This is all the data AhaBox syncs.
  • Data on this device: on the device where you delete your account, all AhaBox data is erased, including XP, Sparks, streaks, game records, favorites, focus sessions, habit and water entries, reminders and settings, and all scheduled AhaBox notifications are cancelled. On other devices, local data stays until you uninstall AhaBox there.
  • Admin summary copies: summary copies of account records used by our internal admin dashboard are removed automatically within 24 hours.

What may be kept

  • Aggregated, anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics) that cannot identify you, kept per Google's retention settings (up to 14 months).
  • Information Meli Labs must keep to meet legal obligations, if any.

Guest (anonymous) accounts hold no name or email, only a generated nickname and progress. Uninstalling AhaBox removes all data on the device; to have a guest record removed from our servers, email us from the app's Settings → Contact.

See also the AhaBox Privacy Policy.
